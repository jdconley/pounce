# Widgets (daily habit PR 6) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** §15 row 6: the `PounceWidgets` extension (`com.conleychaos.play.widgets`) with the Us widget (small, medium, StandBy) and the Streak widget (`accessoryCircular`, `accessoryRectangular`, `accessoryInline`), a partner configuration, the interactive Pounce button, timelines for the predictable changes, reloads from the app and from `PounceNotify`, and WidgetKit push (the token goes to `PUT /v1/devices/me/widget-push`, never from a kitten's device). The App Group `group.com.conleychaos.play` carries the snapshot; a shared keychain access group carries the device credential the widget's fetch and intent need.

**Architecture:** Every rule is pure PounceKit code with Swift Testing tests, under `PounceKit/Sources/PounceKit/Widgets/`: the snapshot the app writes to the App Group (`WidgetSnapshot`, `WidgetSnapshotStore`), the viewer's day and its 04:00 rollover (`WidgetDay`), what a widget shows for a pair at a moment (`UsWidgetState`: cats' poses, paws, ring, mode, tables, the Pounce button, the tap link), the timeline's dates and reload (`WidgetTimeline`), the Pounce intent's send (`WidgetPounce`), the widget push token rule (`WidgetPushToken`), the push payload folded into the snapshot (`WidgetSnapshot.applying(_ push:)`), the widget links (`WidgetLink`), and the mono template art for accented and vibrant modes (`MonoArt`). `APIClient` gains a mode that never mints or clears the credential (the widget must not make a new device), a timeout, and `setWidgetPushToken`. The app's `WidgetBridge` writes the snapshot when what the widgets show changed, reloads, reloads once more on background, and uploads the widget token. The extension holds only SwiftUI views, the provider, the two intents and the push handler, and draws the app's own art from the containing app bundle (as `PounceNotify` does), so PR 8's art swaps reach the widgets with no copy.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 (strict concurrency; must build on CI's Xcode 26.6, checked locally with Xcode 26.2's toolchain), SwiftUI, WidgetKit (iOS 26: `WidgetPushHandler`, `widgetAccentedRenderingMode`), AppIntents, CoreGraphics, Swift Testing, XcodeGen, ImageMagick for placeholders.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-daily-habit-design.md` §11 (11.1, 11.2; 11.3 is PR 7), §14.1 (kittens: widgets refreshed by timelines and the app only, no widget push), §15 row 6, §16. - Kitten (§14.1): the widget push token never leaves the device (neither the app nor the extension uploads it); unknown band counts as kitten. Widgets still refresh by timeline (the widget's own fetch with the device credential is an internal operation) and by the app.

## Decisions where the spec is silent

1. **Two widget kinds.** `us` (systemSmall, systemMedium; StandBy is the small one with no container background) and `streak` (the three accessory families). Both take the same `PartnerIntent` configuration and both adopt the push handler. Gallery names "Us" and "Streak" (names; no descriptions, so no other words).
2. **Partner `nil` means the top pair**, so pinning in the app moves the widget. A partner whose pair is gone falls back to the top pair. The partner list shows each pair's partner name with their cat's face.
3. **The snapshot** (`widgets.json` in the App Group) holds the owner (person id, name, cat), band, server base URL (nil under demos and `-uitest-reset`, so the widget never fetches), every owner pair's `StreakView`, table names for the pairs' tables, the owner's quiet-hours start (for the evening cutoff), the day it was saved for, and local pounce send times. The app writes it only when its widget-visible content changed, and reloads then; on background it reloads once more regardless (§11.2).
4. **Projection without the network.** An entry later than the snapshot's day shows today grey (me, them and kept false); a pause whose last day is past shows no suitcase. The count is left as it was: the server settles it, and the widget never claims a day was missed.
5. **The evening "at risk" pose** starts at the evening cutoff (quiet start minus two hours, §6.1; 20:00 by default) when today isn't kept and the streak isn't paused and is at least 1: whoever isn't in yet is drawn awake and eager. No words, no countdown, never the terrified pose.
6. **Poses** (§11.1, existing sprites): paused both asleep; kept both celebrate; my half done mine celebrate (theirs awake when it's their move, else idle); my move mine awake, theirs idle (facing you); their move mine idle, theirs awake; otherwise both idle. StandBy: `cats-asleep-condo` with the numeral.
7. **Timeline:** entries at now, the evening cutoff, a pounce check's expiry and the next 04:00 rollover (whichever fall before the reload); reload at the earlier of the rollover and three hours from now, so a missed push costs at most three hours. Each reload makes one `GET /v1/me/streaks` with a 5-second timeout and falls back to the snapshot; a fetched list is written back.
8. **The Pounce button** (medium): ready shows the bell and sends a "you pick" pounce to the shown pair's partner; sent shows `pounce-bell-sent` (a moon beside it when quiet) until the server says it was answered or an hour passes from the send (the local send time, else the snapshot time); resting shows the sleeping bell and does nothing; answered shows the answer's picture (their cat leaping, a clock, their cat asleep) for that hour. It reuses `BellState.make` and `PounceStore.send` (429 is resting). A kitten may send (§14.1: in-app pounces to people they play with). Demo snapshots mark the send locally with no network.
9. **Tables on the medium widget:** the pair's `myMove` tables first, then `theirMove`, at most two, named from the snapshot (`TableName.fallback` otherwise).
10. **Taps** use a private `pounce-widget://` link, delivered only by WidgetKit to the containing app (the scheme isn't registered, so nothing else can send it): `pounce-widget://table/<CODE>` rejoins a table, `pounce-widget://us?pair=<id>` opens the Us screen on a pair, `pounce-widget://app` opens the app. Small and rectangular open your first my-move table, else the Us screen; circular opens the Us screen; inline opens the app; medium rows open their table.
11. **`PounceNotify` writes to the snapshot:** a push's `streak` block updates that pair's count, paws, naps and moves (a pair the snapshot lacks is ignored), and a `pounce_answered` sets that pair's answer; then it reloads the widgets.
12. **The widget push token** is uploaded by whichever side sees it first while the band is teen or adult: the extension's `pushTokenDidChange` (with the shared credential), and the app at launch and foreground from `WidgetCenter.shared.currentPushInfo`. The last upload (`{deviceId, token}`) is kept in the App Group so neither sends it twice; a new device credential sends it again. A 404 (server with `NOTIFY` off) is not retried until the token or device changes.
13. **Rendering:** full color has the loud gradient and `widget-bg` container background; accented and vibrant draw the cats and condo as mono template art made on the device from the same sprites (`MonoArt`: alpha from ink darkness, so outlines and shading survive), and the numeral is `widgetAccentable`. The circular ring is a capacity gauge: half for one of you in, full for kept or both in.
14. **Empty widget** (no owner yet, no pairs, or streaks off): your cat (or a generic cat) on the first condo stage with no numeral; a tap opens the app.
15. **Placeholder** (gallery, loading): a fixed sample pair (Mia, 12) that isn't Debug-only.

## Review Focus

1. **The widget must never mint or clear the device credential**, or a 401 in the extension would make a new device under the app. Test: `APIClientTests.testNoMintClientNeverMints`, `testNoMintClientKeepsCredentialOn401`.
2. **A kitten's device never uploads a widget push token**, from either side, and an unknown band counts as kitten. Test: `WidgetPushTokenTests.testKittenNeverUploads`, `testUnknownBandNeverUploads`.
3. **The day boundary**: an entry after 04:00 shows today grey, one at 03:59 doesn't; DST nights; a pause ending; time zones. Test: `WidgetDayTests`, `UsWidgetStateTests.testRolloverGreysToday`, `testPauseEndsAtRollover`.
4. **The Pounce intent** sends once, only from ready, maps 429 to resting, and the check expires after an hour. Test: `WidgetPounceTests`.
5. **A push the extension half-understands** (no `streak`, a pair it doesn't know) never corrupts the snapshot. Test: `WidgetSnapshotTests.testUnknownPairPushIgnored`, `testPushWithoutStreakLeavesSnapshot`.
6. **The app's reloads** happen only on a visible change (plus background), so the budget isn't spent on every refresh. Test: `WidgetSnapshotTests.testVisibleChange`.
