### Description (4,000 max) [2,632]

Pounce is game night that fits into your day. Take your turn when you have a minute, and the game waits for everyone else. When the family is home, put the table on the TV and play together.

FIVE GAMES TO PLAY TOGETHER
Cat Tower is a rummy-style card game where your cats climb a ten-shelf tower. Collect the right cards to clear each level. Yarn is wild. A Cucumber makes the next cat skip a turn.
Tuna Tiles is tile rummy for two to four players. Yarn is wild there too.
Birdwatch is a card game in teams for four to six. Bid for the hand, name trump and win tricks. The Bird is the top trump. Play by Official, Tucker or Friesen rules.

NEW: TOY TUMBLE
Everyone plays at once. Drop toys, clear rows, and toss tricks into each other's boxes. Two to six players. Put it on the TV to see every box.

NEW: PURRTRAIT
A drawing game for the whole room, two to ten players. Start it on the TV, then draw on your phone, and the TV shows it as you draw. Everyone guesses out loud, and the drawer taps who got it first.

THE DAILY DISH
Four new puzzles every day, the same for the whole family. Easy on Monday, harder by Saturday.
Sniff: find the five-letter word in six tries.
Nine Lives: fill the grid so every row, column and box gets 1 to 9 once. Three lives and three free hints.
Cat Nap: one cat in each row, column and patch, and no two cats touch.
Box Fort: drag pieces onto the rug and fill a line to clear it.
A family board shows how far everyone got today, and every puzzle you finish counts toward your streaks.

POUNCE ON THE TV
Put the table on a big screen and gather round. Open Pounce on Apple TV, or play.conleychaos.com/play/tv in a browser on the TV, then scan the code with your phone. Your phone shows just your cards. Tap Table any time to see the whole board. Put tonight's Daily Dish board on the TV too, and show your puzzle there while you solve it.

PLAY YOUR PERSON
Keep a game going with your partner all week. Pounce keeps your streak, and a widget puts it on your Home Screen.

PLAY THE WHOLE FAMILY
Up to six players at a card table, and ten at Purrtrait. Send a link to grandparents across the country, let someone on the couch scan a code, pass one phone around, or mix all three in the same game. Short a player? Seat a House Cat bot.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
A reminder tells you when it's your turn. The live table on your Lock Screen shows whose move it is.

MADE WITH KIDS IN MIND
No chat. No public profiles. No ads. No account needed to play. Children under 13 get a simpler, limited version, and a parent decides whether they get turn alerts.

Privacy policy: https://play.conleychaos.com/privacy
