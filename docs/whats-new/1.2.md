# What's New in Pounce 1.2

Pasted into App Store Connect (the 1.2 version, What's New in This Version). Keep each line short and in words a family member can read.

---

Pounce on the TV. Put the table on a big screen and gather round.

Your phone shows just your cards. Tap Table any time to see the whole board.

Show on TV: scan the code on the TV, or type it.

Pass and play still works, with the cover on your phone.

The Daily Dish: four new puzzles every day, the same for the whole family.

Sniff, Nine Lives, Cat Nap and Box Fort. Easy on Monday, harder by Saturday.

A family board shows how everyone did today. Put it on the TV.

Every puzzle you finish counts toward your streaks.
