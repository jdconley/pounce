# What's New in Pounce 1.4

Draft for App Store Connect (the 1.4 version, What's New in This Version), for the iOS version and the tvOS version. Keep each line short and in words a family member can read.

---

Two new games.

Toy Tumble: drop toys, clear rows and toss tricks at each other, all at once. Two to six players. Put it on the TV to see every box.

Purrtrait: a drawing game for the whole room, two to ten players. Draw on your phone, and the TV shows it as you draw. Everyone guesses out loud, and you tap who got it first.

Nine Lives has three lives and three free hints a day.

The Daily Dish clock counts only while the puzzle is on screen.

A puzzle you don't finish shows how far you got, on the board too.
