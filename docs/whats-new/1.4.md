# What's New in Pounce 1.4

Pasted into App Store Connect (the 1.4 version, What's New in This Version) on 27 November 2026. Only the iOS version has it: tvOS 1.4 is the first Apple TV version on the App Store, which has no What's New. 1.3 never reached the App Store (it was pulled from review for 1.4), so this also says what 1.3 brought: Birdwatch and the Cat Tower fixes. The full listing is in `1.4-listing.md`. Keep each line short and in words a family member can read.

---

Three new games.

Birdwatch: a card game in teams for four to six. Bid, name trump and win tricks. Play by Official, Tucker or Friesen rules.

Toy Tumble: drop toys, clear rows and toss tricks at each other, all at once. Two to six players. Put it on the TV to see every box.

Purrtrait: a drawing game for the whole room, two to ten players. Draw on your phone, and the TV shows it as you draw. Everyone guesses out loud, and you tap who got it first.

Nine Lives has three lives and three free hints a day.

The Daily Dish clock counts only while the puzzle is on screen.

A puzzle you don't finish shows how far you got, on the board too.

Cat Tower fixes from family game night: the screen stays awake during a game, and cards drop where they snap.
