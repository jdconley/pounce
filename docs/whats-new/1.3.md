# What's New in Pounce 1.3

Draft for App Store Connect (the 1.3 version, What's New in This Version). 1.3 is in TestFlight and not submitted yet. Birdwatch has played in the web player and on its TV page since 26 November 2026. Keep each line short and in words a family member can read.

---

New game: Birdwatch. A card game in teams for four to six players.

Bid for the hand, take the feeder's extra cards, name trump and win tricks.

The Bird is the top trump.

With five players, call a card. Whoever holds it is your secret partner.

Play by Official, Tucker or Friesen rules.

House Cats fill any empty seat, and take over for anyone who leaves.

Put it on the TV. Your phone holds just your cards.
