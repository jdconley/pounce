# What's New in Pounce 1.3

Pasted into App Store Connect (the 1.3 version, What's New in This Version). Keep each line short and in words a family member can read. The Daily Dish has been in the browser and the TestFlight build since launch day; 1.3 is the first App Store version with it (`DISH_SINCE` in `apps/play-api/src/http/links.ts`).

---

The Daily Dish: four new puzzles every day, the same for the whole family.

Sniff, Nine Lives, Cat Nap and Box Fort. Easy on Monday, harder by Saturday.

A family board shows how everyone did today. Put it on the TV.

Every puzzle you finish counts toward your streaks.
