# What's New in Pounce 1.3

Pasted into App Store Connect (the 1.3 version, What's New in This Version), for the iOS version and the tvOS version. The full listing is in `1.3-listing.md`. Keep each line short and in words a family member can read.

---

New game: Birdwatch. A card game in teams for four to six players.

Bid for the hand, take the feeder's cards, name trump and win tricks. The Bird is the top trump.

With five players, call a card. Whoever holds it is your secret partner.

Play by Official, Tucker or Friesen rules.

House Cats fill any empty seat, and take over for anyone who leaves.

Put it on the TV. Your phone holds just your cards.

Cat Tower fixes from family game night:

Cards drop where they light up.

A Cucumber's target shows "Pickled".

"Lay down first?" asks before you discard a full level.

The Lay down button says so.

Laying down at a TV table shows you the table.

Cards in your hand are solid and never start small.

The Apple TV stays awake through a game.
