# Copy pass, 12 November 2026

Every user-facing string in the iOS app outside Home and How to play, before and after the copy and fit pass. The rules are in the repo's `AGENTS.md` ("Words in the UI"). Strings are grouped by screen; "(cut)" means the line is gone, and the screen says enough without it. Unchanged strings are listed too, so the table is the whole inventory.

- Strings inventoried: 696
- Changed: 155
- Average length of the changed strings: 51 characters before, 26 after (cuts count as 0)

Not changed on purpose: the two significant-change notices iOS shows a parent or an adult (`SignificantChangeGate.swift`), which describe an approved change in legal terms; the tile game's own rules page (`TilesStage.swift`, How to play, where longer text is allowed); VoiceOver hints that explain a gesture to someone who can't see it; and Home's own views (`HomeView`, `GameCard`, `TableCard`, `StreakStripView`, the sign-in card, the update card and locked-table rows on Home), which the Home redesign rewrote.

Safety and legal strings that were shortened, each still saying what happens: the delete confirmations, the block confirmation, the report picker, the parent consent panel, the consent sheet and its finished step, and the kids' made-up-name note. Marked **(legal)** below.
